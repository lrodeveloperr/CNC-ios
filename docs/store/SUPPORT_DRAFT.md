## Need help with a job record?

For a restore problem, a hold you cannot resolve, or a subscription that does not appear, contact [info@worksbienstudios.com](mailto:info@worksbienstudios.com) with the app version, iOS version, device model and the step where the problem occurred. Please do **not** send confidential drawings, customer identifiers, measurements or full backups unless necessary and authorized.

- **A mismatch hold:** confirm the corrected physical reference, then record a reasoned resolution of its issue. Re-confirmation alone does not clear the issue.
- **An inspection hold:** enter and review every required reading. A rejected inspection stays held until the documented recovery steps are completed.
- **A missing Pro subscription:** open Settings → Upgrade → Restore Purchases, then check that the Apple Account has an eligible active subscription. Apple handles billing and cancellation in its subscription settings.
- **Export and backup:** Records can prepare a CSV, handoff PDF or JSON backup for sharing. An archive is a separate file; keep the file and the matching receipt/backup together. Sharing is your choice.
- **Data deletion:** delete the app using iOS to remove local app data, and delete separately any files you exported or Apple device backups you control. A restored backup can bring records back.

The app cannot read your records remotely or see a machine's measurements. Never share private shop data over email merely to ask a question.
